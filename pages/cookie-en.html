Note: emsarsolutions.az and emsarconsulting.az automatically redirect to emsargroup.az. Cookies are set on the main domain (emsargroup.az) after the redirect.

1. What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) by your browser when you visit a website. Cookies help the website recognise you, remember your preferences and provide a better user experience.

Cookies are not malicious software and cause no harm to your device.

2. Why Do Our Websites Use Cookies?

  • To ensure the website works correctly — essential functionality and navigation
  • To manage domain redirects — emsarsolutions.az and emsarconsulting.az → emsargroup.az
  • To remember your language preference — switching between AZ/EN/RU
  • To measure site performance — learning which pages are most visited via Google Analytics
  • To improve user experience — anonymous statistical analysis
  • To ensure security — preventing spam and malicious activity in forms

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website's core functions to work. Without them, the site will not function correctly.

Cookie name Purpose Duration Domain
language Remember selected language (AZ/EN/RU) 12 months emsargroup.az
session_id Manage user session Session emsargroup.az
csrf_token Form security (CSRF protection) Session emsargroup.az
cookie_consent Remember your cookie consent 12 months emsargroup.az

3.2. Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

Our websites use Google Analytics to analyse user behaviour anonymously. All data is collected in aggregate and anonymous form.

Cookie name Purpose Duration Provider
_ga Uniquely identify users (anonymous ID) 2 years Google Analytics
_ga_<ID> Store session state (GA4) 2 years Google Analytics
_gid Distinguish user behaviour 24 hours Google Analytics
_gat Throttle request rate 1 minute Google Analytics

4. Domain Redirects

  • emsarsolutions.az → 301 redirect → emsargroup.az/solutions

5. Opting Out of Google Analytics

  • Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Browser settings: You can block third-party cookies
  • "Do Not Track" mode: You can enable this in your browser

6. Managing Cookies

6.1. Via Browser Settings

  • Google Chrome: support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
  • Mozilla Firefox: support.mozilla.org/cookies
  • Safari (macOS/iOS): support.apple.com/safari
  • Microsoft Edge: support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge
⚠️ Important note: If you block strictly necessary cookies (e.g. "language" and "session_id"), some website features (language preference saving, correct form functioning) may not work properly. Disabling analytics cookies (Google Analytics) does not affect website functionality.

7. Consent to Cookies

By accessing any of the three domains, you consent to the use of strictly necessary cookies (essential for the website to function). Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) can be managed through your browser settings.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by changing your browser settings.

8. "Do Not Track" Signals

Some browsers allow sending "Do Not Track" signals. There is currently no universal internet standard for how these signals should be interpreted. Our websites do not currently respond to these signals automatically.
