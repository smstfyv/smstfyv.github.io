3. Personal Data Collected

3.1. Data Provided Directly by You

When filling in the "Free Consultation" form or contacting us through other channels:

  • Name and surname — to address you personally
  • Email address — to send a written reply to your request
  • Company name (optional) — to understand the business context
  • Service of interest — to route your request to the appropriate company
  • Information about your project — to prepare a suitable proposal

When contacting us by phone or WhatsApp, your phone number is also collected.

3.2. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • IP address and geographic location (city level)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and device type
  • Visit date, time and duration
  • Pages visited and navigation path
  • Referrer (source of visit)
  • Language selected (AZ/EN/RU)

This technical data is not used to identify you as an individual; it is processed only for aggregate statistical analysis via Google Analytics. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Purposes of Processing Personal Data

  1. Responding to your request — free consultation, price quote, information requests (within 1 business day)
  2. Providing our services — IT solutions, accounting, customs and 3D Studio services
  3. Routing to the appropriate company — between EMSAR SOLUTIONS LLC and EMSAR CONSULTING LLC based on your request
  4. Maintaining customer communication — project discussion, contract preparation, ongoing support
  5. Ensuring website functionality — detecting and resolving technical issues
  6. Statistical analysis — studying website usage via Google Analytics
  7. Fulfilling legal obligations — where required by the legislation of the Republic of Azerbaijan

Your personal data is not sold or rented to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Your consent — by clicking "Send Request" you have given explicit consent
  • Contract performance — within the framework of a concluded or anticipated contract with you
  • Legal obligations — to fulfil obligations before the legislation of Azerbaijan
  • Legitimate interests — to conduct business activities and improve customer service

7. Data Storage Location and Retention Period

8. Your Rights

Under Article 7 of the Republic of Azerbaijan Law "On Personal Data" you have the right to:

  • Obtain information — learn what personal data we hold about you
  • Access — review the content of the data collected
  • Request correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
  • Request deletion — have data deleted, except where required by law
  • Object to processing — object in writing or electronically to the collection and processing of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw your consent at any time
  • Request blocking — request that processing be temporarily suspended
  • File a complaint — contact the competent state authorities of the Republic of Azerbaijan

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@emsargroup.az with the subject "Personal data inquiry". You will receive a response within 30 calendar days.
